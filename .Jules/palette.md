## 2026-10-24 - ARIA pressed state for visual toggles
**Learning:** When buttons are used as toggles or filters and their active state is only conveyed visually (e.g., via background color), screen readers don't know the button's state.
**Action:** Always pair visual selected states on buttons acting as toggles or filters with the `aria-pressed` attribute to ensure accessibility.
